## Learnings and notes
### `+` heavy, content loaded parts
### `-` easy, mind relaxing parts
### `+` first speaker introduces all speakers in the group
### `-` or giving over: previous speaker introduces next speaker
### `+` interface: next speaker (one sentence) summary content of previous speaker (not the intro part)
### `?` learnings from the questions


## Introduce the topic in real life (very simple)
### `+` introduce the problem
### `-` introduce the technique (very short in DS terms)
### `?` hint at the result (teaser)


## Presentation structure
### `+` data description: show the scope of project
### `-` teaser: one interesting finding (very simple)
### `+` approach: explain the project roadmap
### `-` tools: describe used models, parameters etc.
### `+` results: focus on evaluation and visualization
### `-` achievement: explain real life value of solution
### `?` lookout: imagine what would be a business case
